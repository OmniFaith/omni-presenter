# Omni Presenter 0.8.1-alpha

## Highlights

- **Default Monitor Aspect Ratio Matching**: The setup wizard and presentation settings now default to matching the selected monitor's native aspect ratio automatically (`Proporção do monitor selecionado`), with quick presets for 16:9, 16:10, 4:3, and 21:9.
- **Streamlined Media Player & Projection Auto-Close**:
  - The player now automatically ends the active projection when video playback finishes, preventing the presentation window from being left open on a black screen.
  - Smooth fade-out and close animation for the player container upon video completion.
  - Removed the redundant video preview frame from inside the Player panel, focusing playback visualization exclusively in the dedicated Visualização (Stage / Preview) panel.
- **Video Display Fit Modes**:
  - Added an interactive display mode selector directly on the player toolbar, allowing one-click cycling and right-click selection between **Conter** (Contain - preserve full video aspect ratio without cropping), **Cobrir** (Cover - fill projection canvas, cropping edges when aspect ratios differ), and **Estender** (Stretch - stretch video to fill canvas).
  - Mode selections immediately propagate to both Preview and live Projection outputs.
- **Pure Neutral Darker Pro Palette**:
  - Completely eliminated bluish/slate undertones from the Darker Pro dark theme in favor of true monochrome carbon/charcoal neutrals ($R = G = B$).
  - Active toggle switches now feature solid `#505050` tracks with crisp white knobs (`#FFFFFF`), delivering clear 8:1 contrast.
- **Intelligent Text Truncation in Playlist and Library**:
  - Long file and playlist item titles now truncate gracefully with ellipsis (`…`) within available bounds, ensuring text never overlaps the played status toggle icon or bleeds off-panel.
  - Hovering over playlist items reveals their full title and subtitle via native tooltips.
- **Refined Iconography & Setup Flow**:
  - Replaced decorative sparkle icons across setup wizard steps, about modal, design templates, and transitions with contextual symbols (`HAND_WAVING`, `PROJECTOR_SCREEN`, and `PULSE`).
  - Streamlined the setup wizard breaks step for a cleaner onboarding experience.
