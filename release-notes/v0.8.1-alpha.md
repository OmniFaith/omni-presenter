# Omni Presenter 0.8.1-alpha

## Highlights

- **Default Monitor Aspect Ratio Matching**: The setup wizard and presentation settings now default to matching the selected monitor's native aspect ratio automatically (`Proporção do monitor selecionado`), with quick presets for 16:9, 16:10, 4:3, and 21:9.
- **Streamlined Media Player, Performance & Hardware Acceleration**:
  - Added a **Hardware Acceleration** preference in Settings (`Aceleração de hardware na decodificação de vídeo`), enabling FFmpeg hardware decoding (`-hwaccel auto` across VA-API, NVDEC, D3D11, and VideoToolbox) for smoother playback and drastically lower CPU consumption.
  - Unlocked multi-threaded decoding and filtering (`-threads 0`), utilizing available CPU cores rather than restricting playback to 2 threads.
  - Eliminated ~110 MB/s of continuous frame buffer heap allocations by reusing frame memory across decoded frames.
  - Optimized the filter graph to bypass expensive CPU pixel equalizers (`eq`) when contrast and saturation are at their defaults.
  - The player now automatically ends the active projection when video playback finishes, preventing the presentation window from being left open on a black screen.
  - Smooth fade-out and close animation for the player container upon video completion.
  - Removed the redundant video preview frame from inside the Player panel, focusing playback visualization exclusively in the dedicated Visualização (Stage / Preview) panel.
- **Video Display Fit Modes**:
  - Moved the display fit selector to a dedicated, high-contrast segmented control (`[ Conter | Cobrir | Estender ]`) directly below playback controls, keeping the title bar clean and preventing text clipping.
  - One-click selection between **Conter** (Contain - preserve full video aspect ratio without cropping), **Cobrir** (Cover - fill projection canvas, cropping edges when aspect ratios differ), and **Estender** (Stretch - stretch video to fill canvas).
  - Mode selections immediately propagate to both Preview and live Projection outputs.
- **Expanded Media Conversion, Resolutions & Aspect Ratios**:
  - Expanded video conversion formats with **MP4** (H.264/AAC), **WebM** (VP9/Opus), **MKV** (Lossless FFV1/FLAC), and **MOV** (QuickTime).
  - Added video aspect ratio conversion options: **Manter original (Recomendado)**, **16:9**, **16:10**, **4:3**, **9:16 (Vertical)**, and **21:9 (Ultrawide)**.
  - Added video resolution downscaling presets with current source resolution detection and display: **Original (Recomendado)**, **4K (2160p)**, **1080p (Full HD)**, **720p (HD)**, and **480p (SD)**.
  - Expanded audio conversion formats with **MP3** (320 kbps), **FLAC** (Lossless), **WAV** (PCM), **AAC** (M4A), and **OGG** (Vorbis).
  - Added **JPEG** support alongside **PNG** and **WebP** for images.
- **Media Information Inspector & Refined Progress**:
  - Added an **Informações da mídia** context menu action for library media, showing complete dimensions, aspect ratio, duration, codecs, file size, and container details.
  - Fixed the floating conversion toast width to remain compact and sleek, avoiding excessive horizontal stretching.
  - Hovering over the floating conversion toast or actively converting library items now presents rich video metadata (filename, resolution, duration, codecs, and percent).
  - Library item rows actively converting in the background display an inline progress bar and percentage (`45%`), keeping operators informed of progress at a glance.
- **Pure Neutral Darker Pro Palette**:
  - Completely eliminated bluish/slate undertones from the Darker Pro dark theme in favor of true monochrome carbon/charcoal neutrals ($R = G = B$).
  - Active toggle switches now feature solid `#505050` tracks with crisp white knobs (`#FFFFFF`), delivering clear 8:1 contrast.
- **Intelligent Text Truncation in Playlist and Library**:
  - Long file and playlist item titles now truncate gracefully with ellipsis (`…`) within available bounds, ensuring text never overlaps the played status toggle icon or bleeds off-panel.
  - Hovering over playlist items reveals their full title and subtitle via native tooltips.
- **Refined Iconography & Setup Flow**:
  - Replaced decorative sparkle icons across setup wizard steps, about modal, design templates, and transitions with contextual symbols (`HAND_WAVING`, `PROJECTOR_SCREEN`, and `PULSE`).
  - Streamlined the setup wizard breaks step for a cleaner onboarding experience.
